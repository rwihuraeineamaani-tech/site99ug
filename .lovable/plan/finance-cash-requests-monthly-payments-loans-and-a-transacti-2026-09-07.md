# Finance: cash requests, monthly payments, loans and a transaction record

Turn the placeholder Finance section into a working money system that plugs into the rest of the portal. Everything is in UGX.

## Cash requisitions (everyday staff)

- Any team member can raise a cash request: amount, what it is for, date needed, category, optional client/resident or shoot day it relates to, and an optional attachment (quote, receipt, screenshot).
- Route: raised -> Managing Director approves -> a Founder approves -> Finance processes.
- Either approver can decline with a reason; the requester sees it and can re-submit a corrected copy.
- Every person sees a "Waiting on you" entry on their dashboard, matching how content approvals already work.
- Finance can see requests at all times but the "Pay" action only unlocks after both approvals.

## Recurring monthly payments

- Finance sets up a recurring item once: payee, amount, category, which day of the month, internal (staff, retainers) or external (rent, subscriptions), and whether it is active.
- On the 1st of each month the system builds that month's payment run from the active items. Finance can add one-off lines to the run.
- Founders get the run as a single approval: they can approve the whole run or approve/hold individual lines.
- Finance sees the run before approval but cannot mark anything paid until a line is approved.

## Payment board

- A board of everything cleared for payment: approved cash requests plus approved lines from the month's run.
- Columns: To pay -> In progress -> Paid (and Failed/On hold).
- Marking a line paid asks for the method (MoMo, Airtel, bank, cash), the payee reference/number, the date, and an optional proof/invoice upload.
- Filters by month, payee type (internal/external), category and status; CSV export for the month.

## Transactions and audit

- Every payment writes a transaction with an auto-generated ID, e.g. `TXN-2609-0142` (year, month, running number). The ID never changes and cannot be edited or deleted.
- A transaction links to its source (cash request or recurring line), the invoice/proof file, the payee, the approvers and the person who paid.
- Nothing is ever deleted — corrections are recorded as a reversal transaction that points back at the original.
- Every approval, edit and payment writes an audit entry (who, what changed, when). The audit trail is visible on the transaction and cannot be altered.

## Look-up

- A search box that accepts a transaction ID, payee name, invoice number or amount and opens the full record: amount, dates, purpose, who approved at each step, method and reference, attachments, linked resident/shoot, and the full audit trail.

## Loans

- Two kinds: money lent to staff (advances, repaid from pay) and loans between the business and outside parties.
- Each loan records: counterparty, principal, purpose, start date, agreed repayment schedule, and any interest as a flat agreed total (no interest maths beyond a stated figure).
- Repayments are logged against the loan and each one creates a transaction, so the outstanding balance is always live.
- Staff loans show on the person's record; a staff loan needs the same MD + Founder approval before money moves.

## Who sees what

- Any staff: raise a cash request, see their own requests and their own loans.
- MD: first approval on cash requests.
- Founders: second approval on cash requests, approval on the monthly run and on staff loans.
- Finance/Ops: sets up recurring items, runs the payment board, records payments, manages loans, sees all transactions.
- Leadership and Finance: full look-up and exports. No one else sees other people's amounts.

## Technical notes

New tables in `public`, each with GRANTs to `authenticated` and `service_role`, RLS, and `updated_at` triggers:

- `cash_requests` — requester, amount_ugx, purpose, category, needed_on, resident_id/shoot_day_id (nullable), attachment_path, status (`submitted|md_approved|approved|declined|paid|cancelled`), md_approved_by/at, founder_approved_by/at, decline_reason.
- `recurring_payments` — payee_name, payee_kind (`internal|external`), payee_user_id (nullable), amount_ugx, category, day_of_month, active, notes.
- `payment_runs` — month (date, first of month), status (`draft|submitted|approved|closed`), submitted_by/at, approved_by/at.
- `payment_run_lines` — run_id, recurring_id (nullable for one-offs), payee, amount_ugx, category, status (`pending|approved|held|paid`).
- `transactions` — `txn_ref` text unique (generated by a trigger from a per-month sequence table), amount_ugx, direction (`out|in`), source_kind (`cash_request|run_line|loan_disbursement|loan_repayment|reversal`), source_id, payee, method, method_reference, invoice_path, paid_by, paid_at, reverses_txn_id. Update/delete blocked by policy and a trigger; corrections go through reversals.
- `loans` — counterparty_kind (`staff|external`), counterparty_user_id/name, direction (`lent|borrowed`), principal_ugx, agreed_total_ugx, purpose, start_on, schedule_note, status (`pending_approval|active|settled|written_off`), approvals mirroring cash requests.
- `loan_repayments` — loan_id, amount_ugx, due_on, paid_on, transaction_id.
- `finance_audit` — table_name, row_id, action, actor, before/after jsonb, created_at. Written by triggers on the tables above; insert-only, no update/delete policy.

Helper functions (SECURITY DEFINER, `REVOKE EXECUTE` from PUBLIC/anon, `GRANT` to `authenticated`, role-gated inside): `is_md(uuid)`, `approve_cash_request(uuid)`, `decline_cash_request(uuid, text)`, `build_payment_run(date)`, `approve_payment_run(uuid)`, `record_payment(...)` (creates the transaction and stamps the source), `record_loan_repayment(...)`, `finance_lookup(text)`.

A monthly cron job calls `build_payment_run()` on the 1st; Finance can also trigger it manually.

Frontend: `src/pages/app/Finance.tsx` replaces the placeholder with tabs — Requests, Monthly run, Payment board, Loans, Look-up. New `src/pages/app/FinanceTransaction.tsx` at `/app/finance/txn/:ref`. A "Request cash" action is added to the dashboard for all staff, and finance approvals join the existing "Waiting on you" list in `src/pages/app/Dashboard.tsx`. Money rendering uses the existing `Money`/`formatUGX` helpers, and the UI follows the existing surface/rule/`.press` conventions.
